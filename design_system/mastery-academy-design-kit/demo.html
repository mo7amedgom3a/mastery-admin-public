CTA rules

Do

  • One primary per screen or card; it names the outcome: "Enrol now", "ابدأ التعلّم الآن".
  • Pair primary with soft or ghost, primary first in reading order.
  • Keep ink text on coral (6.4:1) and the 2px purple focus ring.
  • Show loading in place: the label hides, width stays.

Don't

  • Draw a border box around a button, add shadows or gradients, or invent radii outside the token scale.
  • Put white text on coral, or a coral button on a coral field.
  • Use vague labels: "Click here", "Submit", "هل ترغب في التعلم؟".
  • Stack two primary buttons side by side.
